How to Play

  1. Each place is worth 1, 2, 4, 8, 16, 32, 64, or 128.
  2. Click a bulb or bit to switch it between 0 and 1.
  3. The decimal number updates as you change the bits.
  4. Turn on Game mode to match a target number.
