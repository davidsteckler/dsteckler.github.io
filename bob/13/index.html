the bus drill<

i went to school

bob came too

it was a bus drill

everyone had to get on the bus

bob looked at the bus driver

the bus driver looked at bob

he licked his lips

bob smiles when he is nervous

the driver said “hop on, little lamb”

bob did not hop on

bob has done the math

the teacher said it was only a drill

the driver had a fork

and a napkin

it was tucked into his shirt

bob practiced the emergency exit

eleven times

from outside the bus

i was hungry

the driver was hungry

we looked at each other

bob walked home

the bus followed him

at four miles an hour

bob is safe>

for now...
