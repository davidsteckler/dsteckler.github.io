ASCII Art & Games

ASCII gives characters codes. People realized those same characters could also become pictures, maps, interfaces, and entire game worlds.

When characters became graphics

ASCII tells the computer what a character means. ASCII art uses those characters as visual building blocks. Rogue took that idea and made the text itself the game world.

How does ASCII art actually work?

The trick is not new characters. It is arranging ordinary text carefully enough that your eyes start seeing shapes, light, and pictures.

// trick 1

Every character needs the same width.

ASCII art depends on a monospace font: every character takes up the same amount of horizontal space.

       /\
      /  \
     /____\
     | [] |
     | __ |
     ||  ||
     ||__||
The columns line up because every character has equal width.
// trick 2

Characters can behave like shades.

Some characters cover very little of the screen. Others are visually dense. Artists can use that difference like light and dark pixels.
