ASCII Art & Games

ASCII gives characters codes. People realized those same characters could also become pictures, maps, interfaces, and entire game worlds.

When characters became graphics

ASCII tells the computer what a character means. ASCII art uses those characters as visual building blocks. Rogue took that idea and made the text itself the game world.

Every “graphic” is a character.

@YouThe player is one character.
$TreasureA dollar sign can become an object.
#CorridorRepeated # characters connect rooms.
DDragonLetters can stand for monsters.
