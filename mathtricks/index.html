How the practice is designed

Worked examples lead into partly completed problems, then recall. Review brings learned tricks back after a delay; mixed questions ask you to recognize the useful method. Visual models and explanation prompts connect the steps to their meaning.

These features draw on the IES learning and study guide and research on interleaved mathematics practice. Review intervals are this site’s practical schedule.
