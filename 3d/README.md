# Highlands trophy design lesson

The `/3d/` page is a self-contained Tinkercad lesson for the Highlands Teacher of the Month trophy.

- The original ten-step tutorial uses an 80 × 50 × 8 mm base.
- The gallery contains 20 dimensioned recipes. These use a 90 × 55 × 20 mm base to fit three lines of award text on the front.
- Each recipe has four front-view diagram stages: separate shapes, overlap, select, and group. Depth is specified in the parts table.
- The Merge blocks lesson demonstrates joining three boxes into an H and cutting a window with a Hole Box.
- The diagrams illustrate construction; they are not a CAD editor or a source of printable geometry. Students build their own models in Tinkercad.
- Progress and designer notes use browser local storage. Submission happens through the teacher's Schoology assignment.

When editing recipes, keep the dimensions, elevations, overlap descriptions, and diagrams consistent. Cut rings before joining them to the rest of a trophy. Inspect actual model connections and the sliced preview before printing.
