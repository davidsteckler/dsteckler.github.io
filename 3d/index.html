    Learn this before picking a design

    Turn simple blocks into a new shape.

    In Tinkercad, overlap solid shapes and Group them to add material. Group a Hole with a solid to remove material. Step through both operations below.

    01 / Put the shapes in the right place

    Give the solids a real overlap.

    Resize the shapes. Use Shift + click to select them, then Align (L) for the directions you need. After opening Align, click the shape that should stay still to use it as the reference. Move a join a little inside its neighbor. Matching depth keeps these flat award shapes on the same front-to-back line.

    02 / Select, then group

    Combine the selected shapes.

    1. Click empty space to clear the previous selection.
    2. Hold Shift and click each shape you want to combine. You can also drag a selection box around just those shapes.
    3. Click Group in the toolbar or press Ctrl + G.
    4. To revise the parts, use Ungroup: Ctrl + Shift + G.

    03 / Use Hole deliberately

    Cut only the part you mean to cut.

    Place the cutter, change it to Hole, select it with its target solid, and Group. For a ring handle, cut the inner circle out of the outer circle before joining that ring to the trophy. Check every view afterward.

    ↔
    A group can still contain separated pieces.

    Grouping keeps the selected shapes together as a group. Physical connections come from overlapping geometry. Look from the side, and check that a Hole has not cut through a connection you need.

    Build it in Tinkercad

      How do I place a block from these numbers?
      1. Reset the workplane to the main grid. Select the part and base together, press L, then click the base once more to use it as the fixed reference. Click the two horizontal center dots. Leave vertical alignment alone.
      2. Click empty space, then select only the part. In Top view, set Snap Grid to 1 mm. Use the arrow keys to move it the stated distance left or right of center. Each tap moves one grid step.
      3. Use the black lift handle’s number to set the part’s bottom elevation. Recheck from the front and side before grouping.

      Shape recipe — millimeters

      Part / shapeW × D × HBottom / position

      W = width, D = front-to-back depth, H = height. Bottom elevation is measured from the main grid. “Center” means centered across the base. Parts share a depth center unless a note says otherwise.

      Finish every design: Use the shared 90 × 55 × 20 mm base in this gallery, then place HIGHLANDS / TEACHER OF / THE MONTH as three lines of raised text on its front face. Aim for about 5 mm visible letter height and 1.5 mm outward thickness; sink the backs 0.5 mm into the base. Keep all lettering inside the face. These gallery bases are taller than the starter’s base to make room for the wording. Follow for the method, then inspect and export your own model.

      Recipes are starting dimensions. Recheck the model in Tinkercad, especially when changing size or rotating shapes. Your teacher reviews orientation, supports, and the sliced model before printing.

      Workplane, lift, and drop

      W lets you place a workplane on a face. New shapes appear on that face. D drops a selected shape onto the active workplane. The black lift handle changes elevation; the white square handles change dimensions. Reset the workplane to the empty grid before checking the whole trophy.

      Solid, Hole, and Group

      A Solid adds material. A Hole removes material when grouped with an overlapping solid. Use Hole only when you want a real cutout. Grouping disconnected solids can still leave disconnected printed pieces. Inspect the joins.

      Something looks wrong?

      My text is lying on the grid.

      Delete or move that text out of the way. Press W, click the upright plaque’s front face, and drag a fresh Text shape onto that face. Its letters should face you. Reset the workplane to the empty grid when finished.

      My plaque floats above the base.

      Reset the workplane to the main grid. Select only the plaque and set its bottom elevation to 7 mm with the black lift handle. The 8 mm base then overlaps it by 1 mm. Use Side view to check.

      Align moves my parts in the wrong direction.

      Undo. Select the base and plaque together, press L, and click only the two center dots for the horizontal directions. The vertical center dot changes their height. Leave the base on the grid and the plaque at 7 mm elevation.

      My model turned one color when I grouped it.

      That is normal. You can use the group’s Solid color options to show multicolor if available. Tinkercad colors are a design preview; your teacher chooses actual filament colors in the slicer. STL does not preserve them.

      The lettering is too small or runs off the edge.

      Use separate Text objects for HIGHLANDS, TEACHER OF, and THE MONTH. Fit each line to no more than 52 mm wide, with about 5–6 mm letter height where possible. Use a simple bold font. If a line is still tiny, shorten the line layout or enlarge the plaque with teacher approval.

      I cannot find my model.

      Select a shape and press F to fit it in view, or use the Home view control near the ViewCube. Check that you did not zoom far past the workplane.
