Why the powers start at zero

The rightmost place is 100 = 1. Moving one place left multiplies its value by 10. The next places are 101, 102, and 103. Change a digit above to see how much it contributes.

The ones, tens, hundreds, and thousands places are powers of ten. The same place-value rule also works in binary, octal, and hexadecimal.

For example, decimal 203 means 2 × 100 + 0 × 10 + 3 × 1.
