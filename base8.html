Why the powers start at zero

The rightmost place is 80 = 1. Moving one place left multiplies its value by 8. The next places are 81, 82, and 83. Change a digit above to see how much it contributes.

Octal groups binary bits in threes. Three bits have 2³ = 8 possible patterns, so one octal digit can stand for three binary bits.

For example, octal 17 means 1 × 8 + 7 × 1 = 15 in decimal.
