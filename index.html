I’m David Steckler, a 7th grade computer science teacher. I build lessons, tools, games, and experiences that help students become more capable, more curious, and less dependent on someone else telling them the next step.

This site is where I keep the work: what I teach, what I build, and the ideas underneath both.

// what I believe

A few things guide almost everything I make.

The longer version lives on the About page. These are the ideas you’ll see repeated in the work itself.

Useful struggle

Learning gets stronger when you try, fail, notice what happened, adjust, and go again.

Less helpless

Students can do more than they think. My job is to help them build evidence of that for themselves.

Real work

Computer science is a way to create, solve problems, understand systems, and make ideas visible.
