A little help before you play

Technology includes tools, materials, and systems people design to solve problems or meet needs.

AI is a type of technology. It can recognize speech and images, make predictions, or generate content. Many modern AI systems use patterns learned from training examples.

Judge the specific feature on each card. A device can contain several kinds of technology.
