I teach computer science. I want kids to figure things out for themselves.

I’m David Steckler. I teach 7th grade computer science, and I spend a lot of time trying to make class feel like a place where kids actually do things. Code, robotics, AI, games, websites, weird little challenges — whatever gets them thinking and building instead of just waiting for the next direction.

This site is basically where I keep the stuff I’m working on. Some of it is for class. Some of it is me messing around with an idea until it works.

// what I believe

A few things I keep coming back to.

I don’t really have a big teaching manifesto. These are just the ideas that keep showing up in how I teach.

01
Useful struggle

I don’t want to rescue kids the second something gets hard. A lot of learning happens in the part where you’re stuck and have to try something else.

02
Less helpless

A big goal for me is pretty simple: students should need me less over time. They should start trusting themselves to figure things out.

03
Build real things

I like when the work turns into something you can actually see, use, play, explain, or show someone.
