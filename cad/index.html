A GOOD PLACE TO START

Your studio, at a glance.

A workspace that grows with you

Beginner keeps the everyday tools and a short guide close at hand. Advanced adds alignment, distribution, mirrored copies, patterns, model measurements, orthographic views, and wireframe display. Switch modes without changing your model.

Build a model

Add a shape, then set its dimensions in the properties panel. Select Sketch to draw a rectangle, circle, or polygon on the ground plane and extrude it to a height. Advanced mode also offers front and side sketch planes with an offset.

Shift + click selects multiple bodies. Union joins them, Subtract cuts the other selected bodies out of the first one, and Intersect keeps their shared volume.

Move around

Drag empty space to orbit. Right drag to pan. Scroll or pinch to zoom. Use the view buttons for Top, Front, Right, or 3D.

Shave a shape

Choose Mold, set the brush size and shave depth, and drag directly on a shape. The tinted preview shows the cut; release to apply. Each stroke cuts into the surface where you started. Choose Cut all the way through for holes and notches. Erasing is limited to five short strokes per batch. Keep cuts & continue keeps the model and clears Undo history before the next batch. A detail limit stops further erasing on complex shapes. Recent Undo history also has a memory limit. Done returns to normal camera controls.

Add details and repetition

Text opens a preview. Type your words and press Enter to create solid lettering. With a text body selected, Text edits its words. With one unlocked body selected, raised or engraved lettering is centered on its top-facing XY surface. Adjust or reposition standalone text like any other body. Rounded boxes and tubes have editable radius or wall controls in Properties.

Advanced alignment keeps the first selected body fixed. Distribution spaces body centers evenly. A pattern creates repeated copies, including the original in its total count. Mirroring adds a reflected copy; originals remain.

Import STL opens a local triangle mesh as a new body. STL does not record units: choose millimeters or inches in Advanced before importing. Files stay in your browser.

Keep what you make

Changes autosave in this browser on this device. Save project downloads an editable file you can open here later or move to another computer. Browser data can be cleared, so download important work.

Export STL downloads visible bodies as a triangle mesh using millimeters. Check the model and its scale in your slicer before printing. This is a mesh modeling studio; it does not include Shapr3D’s full CAD kernel or sketch constraints.
