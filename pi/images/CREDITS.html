Photo and result-preview credits

Each of the 26 project previews combines a distinct finished result with annotations. Terminal output, sorted files, and Pygame screens were produced by running the reference programs. Hardware output art (timings, color mixtures, sample readings, waveform, motion mask, and robot route) was drawn in Python. Example readings are illustrative; they are not measurements from a physical classroom build.

Hardware photographs are reproduced from the sources below. Images have been resized and converted to WebP; the ultrasonic sensor image is cropped from the robotics-parts photograph. SVG labels and leader lines are drawn separately over the photographs.

Except the CamJam night-light composite identified below, Raspberry Pi and Raspberry Pi Foundation photographs and the corresponding annotated adaptations are offered under CC BY-SA 4.0. The RGB LED photograph comes from Jeff Geerling’s repository, under its MIT license. Pygame captures were produced by executing this site's Python reference programs.
