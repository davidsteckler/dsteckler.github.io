Plan for one class per case. Investigate with a partner, record your evidence, and finish with a reflection. Progress saves on this browser. Teacher setup

Teacher setup / printable physical start cards

Print these two cards and hide them in different locations. Students open the corresponding mission and enter the access word they find. You can also use “Try digital preview” during testing. Use the cards for a physical start, or choose the digital start on screen. All later evidence is inside the investigation.

42-minute class plan

0–4: Introduce the case and find the start card. 4–28: Investigate four locks (about six minutes each). Partners switch driver and evidence recorder after every lock. 28–35: Finish the case and compare explanations. 35–42: Write the reflection and submit a screenshot plus the copied case report in Schoology.

Ask: What did you try? What happened? What will you test next? Offer the first hint before giving the solution. Fast finishers can test an alternative route or describe a failure the model does not handle.

Before class

Open each case once on a student device. Hide the start cards only in accessible classroom locations. On shared computers, reset the case before the next student starts. Reflections and notes save locally; the site does not send work to you automatically.

Answer key

Invisible Computer: 30; ABED or ACED; VENT; OFF ON ON. Mission Control: F R F F (other valid routes accepted); 13; <; 12. These are simplified classroom models. The sensor outlier is suspicious, not proven faulty; two agreeing computers can still share a bug.
