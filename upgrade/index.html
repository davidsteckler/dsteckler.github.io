Upgrade the world.

Ten ideas for the choices you make every day.

Lex Fridman #27610 principles
LIVE MOTION
// ten principles

What you do adds up.

01 / 10

Focus your energy.

Give something important your full attention.

02 / 10

Guard your time.

Decide what deserves your time.

03 / 10

Train your mind.

Ask questions. Practice. Stay with something difficult.

04 / 10

Train your body.

Move. Sleep. Give yourself time to recover.

05 / 10

Think for yourself.

Know why you believe what you believe.

06 / 10

Curate your friends.

Spend time with people who help you grow.

07 / 10

Curate your environment.

Make room for the habits you want.

08 / 10

Keep your promises.

Let people count on what you say.

09 / 10

Stay cheerful and constructive.

Look for something you can make better.

10 / 10

Upgrade the world.

Help someone. Build something. Leave your part of the world better.

Source

Michael Saylor on Lex Fridman Podcast #276. The ten pieces of advice begin around 3:38:00.
